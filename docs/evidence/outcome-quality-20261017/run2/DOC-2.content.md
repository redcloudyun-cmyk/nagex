# Working-Model Options Comparison

The table below outlines the key parameters associated with each of the three proposed pilot options for the 30-member team over the 12-week duration:

| Option | Description | Duration | Cost | Risk Level |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Option A** | Hybrid (3 office days) | 12 weeks | $18,000 | Low |
| **Option B** | Fully Remote | 12 weeks | $9,500 | Medium |
| **Option C** | Office Only | 12 weeks | $24,000 | Low |

# Evaluation and Recommendation

Based on the comparative analysis of cost, risk, and structural flexibility:

- **Option C** presents the highest financial investment at $24,000 while maintaining a low risk profile. However, it lacks the flexibility that modern working models often provide, making it a less adaptable choice for the team.
- **Option B** is the most cost-effective solution at $9,500 for the 12-week period. Despite its financial advantage, it carries a medium risk level, which may introduce coordination or engagement challenges for a 30-person team.
- **Option A** strikes a balanced middle ground. With a moderate cost of $18,000 and a low risk profile over the 12-week timeframe, it preserves face-to-face collaboration opportunities while keeping expenses well below the office-only alternative.

**Recommendation:** Option A (hybrid, 3 office days) is recommended for the 12-week pilot. It securely manages operational risk while providing a structured, collaborative environment for the 30-person team without incurring the maximum overhead of an office-only setup.
